GOOD TO KNOW

Clear answers. No surprises.

Can I top up a friend without their Access Key?

Yes. Enter their X username and pay from your own signed-in website account. The username must have a verified paid bot purchase and an active key. We do not disclose their key, email or private balance. If more than one key qualifies, the newest active purchased key receives the credits.

Does 1 credit always mean 1 posted comment?

One credit is reserved when an AI reply is generated. AI provider errors and eligible failed-post attempts are refunded; unused reservations return after 30 minutes. Posting can still fail because of X restrictions or connection problems. Repeated failed-post refunds are limited to prevent abuse.

What if payment verification fails?

Check that you used Binance Pay, USDT, the correct recipient and the exact order amount. Copy the payment Order ID from your Pay history. Do not pay again just because verification is pending. Retry verification or contact support with your order details. Never share your Access Key publicly.

Can I still use My API?

Yes. My API replies do not use Fix Credits. Your API provider may bill you separately. There is no automatic switch to Fix Credits if your API key fails.
