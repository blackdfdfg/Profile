A little clarity

Are these AI text tokens?

No. Fix Credits are reply credits. A generated reply reserves one credit. If the automated comment fails, the extension requests a refund. Provider errors are refunded automatically. Unused reply reservations are returned after 30 minutes. Repeated failed-post refunds are limited to prevent abuse.

Can I buy an unusual quantity?

Yes. Buy any whole quantity starting at 500, for example 734 or 2,137. The total is calculated exactly using the price for your quantity.

Where do the purchased credits appear?

In the extension, choose Fix Credits and press Reload beside Top up. The balance also updates when replies are generated or failed posts are refunded.

Can a friend top up my key?

Yes. They enter and check your purchased Access Key, log in to their own website account, and pay the exact order amount. Credits are added to your key. Your key ownership and X username do not change. Only share your key with someone you trust, never post it publicly.

Can I keep using my own API?

Yes. Select My API in the extension. Your own API replies do not use Fix Credits.
