Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

What we collect

When you create an account we store your email, your name (optional) and a securely hashed password — or your Google account email if you sign in with Google. When you buy a key we store the plan, amount, Binance Order ID and the X username you enter for the key.

Why

We use this only to deliver your Access Key, show your keys and receipts in your dashboard, check that a key is used with the X username it was bought for, and help you if you contact support.

The extension

The extension sends only your Access Key and X username to our server to check the key. Your AI API key and your X data stay in your own browser.

Security

Passwords are hashed (PBKDF2) and never stored in plain text. Payments are checked directly with Binance; we never see your Binance login. The site is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We use Cloudflare (hosting), Google (optional sign-in) and Binance (payment checks) only to run the service.
